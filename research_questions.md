
# LUMA Research Questions

## Main Research Question

Can a budget-aware adaptation policy improve the
accuracy-computation trade-off of few-shot learning
on previously unseen tasks?

## Initial Hypothesis

A policy that considers task uncertainty, support-set
size, and adaptation cost can select between different
adaptation strategies more efficiently than a fixed
strategy.

## Initial Methods to Compare

1. Nearest-neighbor classification
2. Prototypical classification
3. Linear classifier adaptation
4. LUMA adaptive selection policy

## Evaluation Metrics

- Classification accuracy
- Adaptation time
- Computational cost
- Performance under distribution shift
- Variance across random seeds

## Important Constraint

No performance improvement will be claimed without
controlled experiments and reproducible results.
